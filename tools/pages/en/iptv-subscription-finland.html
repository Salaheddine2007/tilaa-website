What an IPTV subscription Finland plan includes

Every IPTV subscription Finland plan has the same content: Finnish and Nordic channels in HD, 4K where the channel broadcasts it, a TV guide and help with the installation on WhatsApp. Only the length and the number of screens change between plans.

All prices for 1, 2 and 3 screens

A longer plan always gives a lower price per month.

How to pay for your IPTV subscription

You order on WhatsApp and get the payment details there. We accept Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal, and from other EU countries also SEPA, iDEAL, Bancontact and giropay. We do not store card details and nothing renews on its own.

Choosing an IPTV subscription Finland households can rely on

When you compare an IPTV subscription Finland sellers offer, look at the contract, the renewal and the support, not just the channel count. Which plan should you choose?

  • Not sure yet: one month. It does not renew, so there is nothing to cancel.
  • Lowest cost per month: twelve months.
  • Several people watching at once: pick the 2 or 3 screen tier.

Questions about plans

Can I change the number of screens later?

Yes. Write to us on WhatsApp before your period ends and say how many screens you want next time.

What happens when the period is over?

The service stops. Nothing renews automatically, and we do not store your card details. If you want to continue, you order again.
