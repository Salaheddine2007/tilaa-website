Install IPTV: Smart TV, Fire TV, Apple TV and phone

You do not need special equipment. You need an app for your device, your login and a connection that is fast enough.

Short answer

To install IPTV you pick a plan, get your login on WhatsApp and put an IPTV app on your TV, box or phone. It takes a few minutes if you have 10 Mbit/s per screen and the right app for your model.

To install IPTV on a TV, a box or a phone you follow the same three steps on every device. The details differ by brand, which is why we ask for your model before we recommend an app.

Before you install IPTV: check your internet

Plan for at least 10 Mbit/s per screen for HD and 25 Mbit/s for 4K. Measure on the TV or the box, not on your phone, because the TV is often further from the router. A network cable is almost always steadier than wifi.

Three steps to install IPTV

  1. Order and get your login. Write to us on WhatsApp with the plan you want and your TV or box model.
  2. Install the app. Open the app store on your device, install the IPTV player we recommend for your model and open it.
  3. Log in. Enter the details we send you. The channel list and the TV guide load within a minute or two.

Install IPTV on Samsung and LG Smart TV

Most models from roughly 2018 and later can run an IPTV app from the TV's own app store. Which apps are available depends on the model and year, so send us the model number from the label on the back.

Install IPTV on Fire TV Stick and Android TV

These give the widest choice of apps. Open the app store, search for the player we recommend and install it. Amazon explains the basics on its Fire TV help pages.

Install IPTV on Apple TV, iPhone and iPad

Install an IPTV player from the App Store, then log in with the details we send. Apple's own Apple TV support pages cover how to install apps.

Install IPTV on a MAG box or a computer

A MAG box is linked to your account with the box's MAC address, and we set that up for you. On a computer you can use VLC or an IPTV app.

If the picture stutters after you install IPTV

Move the TV to a network cable, restart the router and close other streaming on the network. If it still stutters, send us a photo of the screen on WhatsApp.

Tips for a steadier picture

Place the router in the same room as the TV if you can, and keep it away from thick walls. If you must use wifi, choose the 5 GHz band, which is faster over short distances than 2.4 GHz. Close other apps that stream video while you test, and restart the app once after the first login so the channel list and the guide load cleanly.

How long the setup takes

With the right app for your model, most people finish within a few minutes. Finding the right app takes longer than the setup itself, so tell us your model first and we point you to the right one.

What you need before you start

  • Your TV or box model, from the label or the settings menu.
  • An internet connection of at least 10 Mbit/s per screen.
  • Your login from us on WhatsApp.
  • An account in the device's app store.
