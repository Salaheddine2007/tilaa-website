About Tilaa IPTV

An independent IPTV service for households in Finland, with support on WhatsApp.

Tilaa IPTV sells IPTV subscriptions with Finnish and Nordic channels. We have no affiliation with the TV companies, apps or manufacturers mentioned on this site. Ordering, payment and help all happen on WhatsApp.

How we write the guides

The guides are written by the support team, the same people who answer on WhatsApp. We write about questions customers actually ask. Settings and app features are checked against the manufacturers' own help pages, which we link under each guide, and we correct the text when something changes.

What we will not claim

We do not publish invented numbers such as uptime percentages, review scores or channel counts we cannot prove. When we do not know something, we say so. The channel line-up can change over time, so we ask you to check a specific channel with us before you order.
